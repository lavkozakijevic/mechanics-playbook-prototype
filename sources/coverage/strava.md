# Coverage report — Strava

**Mechanics held back:** none. All ten applied tags, Streak, Challenge, Achievement, Milestone, Leaderboard, Comparative Rank, Social Feed, Group Membership, Shareable Win and Profile Completion, had enough on their own to pass the friend test and got a block.

**What's missing, and what to capture on the next walk-through:**

1. **Club collective state (O75, O76).** Whether a club's activities, stats and insights tabs hold a shared, club-level state or just list members' own records decides Group Membership versus Clan/Guild for this product, and it's currently unresolved. Capture: open a populated club's activities and stats tabs.
2. **Segment leaderboard population (O52, O79, O57).** Local Legends, Top 10 and the KOM/CR lists were all empty on this account. Capture: a session on an account with real segment efforts, or a subscribed account.
3. **Coach-authored training guidance (O17, O86, O40).** Whether coaching content lives inside Strava or only inside Runna decides whether Expert Guidance ever applies to Strava itself. Capture: a subscribed session that starts a plan or a workout.
4. **Subscription-gated progress features (O56, O102, O57).** Performance predictions, goals, relative effort, a training log, advanced training analysis and best efforts were all named in paywall copy and never reached; several could carry their own mechanics. Capture: a subscribed session.
5. **Group challenges (O55, O80).** Creating one and the Active tab are both paywalled; how contribution is counted and whether the group is a club or an ad hoc set of friends is unknown. Capture: a subscribed session that creates one.
6. **Streak continuity rule (O107, O108).** What qualifies a week, what a missed week does to the count, and whether any protection exists were never observed, the account only ran four days. Capture: a session on an account with a running streak, recorded after a gap.
7. **Challenge reward identity (O53).** The Hoka Speedgoat 7 Vert Challenge names a reward without saying what it is. Capture: that challenge's own page, or a completed instance.
8. **Subscription terms, internally inconsistent (O96, O99, O100, O104).** Trial length, reminder timing, the charge date and the annual price all conflict across placements. Capture: the "see all plans" screen, and a trial run to its actual end.
9. **Sport count, stated four different ways (O9, O11, O21, O23).** Over 30, 48, 30+, and roughly 40 from the selector itself. Capture: a full count from the sport selector's own list.
10. **April 10 Days Active success condition (O53).** Described in the session both as ten days active and ten days in a row; the screen wording isn't recoverable from the transcript. Capture: that challenge's own page.
11. **No app-store review prompt observed (O114, dropped from the page).** An absent prompt doesn't change what a user can do, so it didn't earn a place in the section pages, but it's worth confirming on a longer-running account whether one exists anywhere, since two short sessions may simply not have reached it.
12. **Self-Set Recurring Target (Pass three, held).** The suggested weekly goal is a strong candidate for a new library entry, a user-set target against a product-defined recurring period that resets rather than completing, but the reset itself was never observed in either Strava or FitOn, the second recorded instance. Capture: a session, in either product, that carries a set target across a period boundary.

**Any other gap worth filling:** A second walkthrough on a more mature Strava account, one with real history, connections and a subscription, would settle nearly everything on this list at once.
