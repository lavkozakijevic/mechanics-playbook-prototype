- The block message states a remaining time rather than a clock time (tier: directly observed).
- Whether the reset is a fixed daily boundary or a rolling 24 hours from the first opening is not established (tier: unresolved).

# Pass three: proposed new tags

### Matched Swap Request

**Draft definition.** A matched swap request lets a user post a standing offer to exchange specified items from their own holdings for specified items they lack, and the product, rather than the user, searches other users' requests for a counterpart and completes or proposes the reciprocal exchange when one is found.

**Source observations:** O25, O22, O10, O26

**Conditions it appears to depend on:** Items that can be transferred between users; a collection that produces duplicates; a pool of other users with open requests; a limit on how many requests one user can hold open.

**Why it is not covered.** Gifting excludes reciprocal exchanges. Set Collection lists trading only as a non-required condition of the collection. Group Membership covers the team scope of some requests but not the exchange itself.

**Recurrence elsewhere:** The pattern is described in general terms that apply to any product where users hold duplicate collectible items; no other analysis in this workspace was consulted to confirm it.

**Caveat.** Held open. No match and no completed exchange were observed, so whether the exchange completes automatically or asks the user to confirm is not established, and that is the mechanic itself rather than a detail around it. Two pieces of evidence would settle it: a session that posts a request and sees it matched, showing what the product does at the moment a counterpart is found; and a second app carrying the same shape, a product-run search of other users' standing offers that completes or proposes the reciprocal exchange.

### Product Scan Reward

**Draft definition.** A product scan reward grants an in-product benefit when the user points the device camera at a recognized physical item, such as a sponsor's product or a physical edition of the product's own content, with the grant limited per item or per period.

**Source observations:** O29, O30, O14, O21

**Conditions it appears to depend on:** Camera access; physical items the product can recognize; a benefit held in the product; a limit on repeat scans.

**Why it is not covered.** Rewarded Advertisement excludes benefits granted for using or purchasing the promoted offering. Earning Tasks requires disclosed effort, a catalogue separate from the core activity and a continuation layer. Daily Login Rewards requires access alone.

**Recurrence elsewhere:** Stated in general terms applicable to products tied to physical goods or packaging; no other analysis in this workspace was consulted to confirm it.

**Caveat.** Held open. In this app the scan sits inside a sponsorship, and a commercial arrangement is not a tag. One app cannot settle whether the scan-to-unlock pattern holds without a sponsor behind the scanned item, or whether it is a form the sponsorship takes. What would settle it is a second app in which the scanned item is the product's own rather than a sponsor's, and scanning it grants an in-product benefit under a repeat limit. The per-item versus per-category limit was also not settled in this session.
