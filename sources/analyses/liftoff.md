# Pass three: proposed new tags

### Predicted Goal Date

**Draft definition.** A predicted goal date is a date the product states for when the user will reach a named target state that the product itself assigns and will recognize when reached, derived from inputs the user has just supplied and presented as a prediction with a rising visual from the present to that date.

**Source observations:** O17, with O15 and O16 supplying the inputs and O48 following it.

**Conditions it appears to depend on:** A present state measured or self-reported during onboarding (O16). A named target state in an ordered structure the product maintains and assigns (O19, O32). **Why it is not covered.** Future Projection covers a computed state that the product never recognizes as reached, such as a position forty years out that lies outside the product's own accounting.
