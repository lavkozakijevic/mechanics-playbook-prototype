**App version:** None.

**Analysis date:** 15 Sep 2026

**Last updated:** 15 Sep 2026

**Source.** This analysis is built from two Granola walkthrough sessions, retrieved as full transcripts; no Granola summary or note was used. The first, "Fiton" (25 Feb 2026), is live narration from first launch through the permission requests, account creation, the personalization questionnaire, the plan choice, the reminder and calendar steps, the first workout and everything that follows it, each of the five home tabs, the meal plan setup, the profile and the settings connections. The second, "Fiton app achievements review" (29 Apr 2026), is a return to the app two months later, stated as focused on achievements, and covers the reopening sequence, the streak control, the weekly progress screen, the profile and the achievements catalogue. Both transcripts are machine speech-to-text of one narrator, labelled "Me" in the first and "Speaker A" in the second. Numbers, prices and names are reported as transcribed; several brand and person names are phonetic in the transcript and are quoted as transcribed rather than corrected, and fragmented passages are marked at the relevant observation. The 29 Apr session observes the app as it stood on that date, and observations drawn from it say so. The app version is not stated in either session and is not inferred.
